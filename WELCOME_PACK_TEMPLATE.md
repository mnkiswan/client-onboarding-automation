# Welcome pack template (Zoho Writer merge template)

Fields in `«»` are merge fields filled by Zoho Flow.

---

# Welcome aboard, «First Name»!

Thank you for choosing us. This welcome pack confirms the onboarding details for «Company Name».

Service package: «Service Package»
Kickoff date: «Start Date»
Your goals: «Project Goals»

## What happens next

Your account manager will contact you within one business day to schedule a kickoff call.

You will receive access to your shared project workspace, where your onboarding tasks are already set up.

At kickoff, we will review your goals together and confirm your first milestones.

We're glad to have you with us.

---
